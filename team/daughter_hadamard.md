\# Daughter Hadamard Gate Notes



\## What is a Hadamard Gate?



The Hadamard Gate is one of the most important quantum gates.



Symbol:



H



It places a qubit into superposition.



\## What Happens?



Before:



|0>



After H:



(|0> + |1>) / √2



This means the qubit can represent both states until measured.



\## Why is it Important?



The Hadamard Gate:



\- Creates superposition

\- Starts many quantum algorithms

\- Helps quantum computers process many possibilities



\## Classical Comparison



Classical bit:



0 or 1



Quantum bit after H:



0 and 1 simultaneously
